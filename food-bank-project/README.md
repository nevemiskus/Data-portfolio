# Food Insecurity Analysis with Food Bank of Northeast Georgia using R and Excel 

# Objective
Analyzing demographic and economic data across 15 counties in Northeast Georgia to identify food insecurity trends among children and seniors

# Tools Used
Excel, R

# Data
Provided datasets from the Food Bank of Northeast Georgia, public datasets 

# Analysis Steps
- Cleaned and organized county data
- Analyzed trends among counties 
- Identified factors influencing trends including school district funding, veteran status, proximity to resources 

# Key Insights
- Correlation found between veteran status and number of seniors below poverty level within counties 
- While urban areas like Athens-Clarke County have better public transportation and physical access to food sources, they still experience high rates of food insecurity, suggesting accessibility extends beyond proximity to food sources
- Counties with high school district enrollment but low per-student funding showed particularly high rates of child food insecurity, suggesting educational resource allocation may play a crucial role in addressing youth hunger

# Output
- Visualization charts and graphs using R
- Summary report of findings
